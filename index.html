About us

We're Liam and Lee, and Leeam Applications is our app studio, based in Northamptonshire, UK.

We met in 2004, hanging around the same housing estate with the same group of mates. We clicked straight away: same sense of humour, always up for a laugh, and never too proud to make fools of ourselves. Lee passed his driving test first and we drifted for a while, until we ended up working at the same warehouse. Before long we were out clubbing together, then off on lads' holidays to Magaluf. Somewhere along the way we stopped being mates and became brothers from other mothers.

Over the years we talked about starting a business more times than we can count. We even researched opening a bar in Magaluf, right down to what it would take. It never got past the idea stage. Then life slowed us down, and we both went through a tough time with our health.

Coming out the other side, we met up for breakfast one morning and got talking about building an app. That conversation became Leeam Applications. The name is just the two of us mashed together: Lee, with "am" on the end, which sounds a lot like Liam. Add the applications, and there you have it.

We're not traditional developers. We taught ourselves from scratch and build with the help of AI. One of us works entirely by screen reader and one of us doesn't, so everything we make gets tried both ways from day one. Losing sight showed one of us how often apps and websites leave blind and visually impaired people out. So that's where we start, but we build for everyone.

Our aim is simple: apps that genuinely help people, and that anyone can use. Every idea and every decision is made by both of us, usually over a weekly catch-up and plenty of jokes.

Liam

I'm Liam. I spent my career in warehouse logistics management, always looking to move up, and what I loved most was helping other people progress too.

Type 1 diabetes changed all that. In January 2022 I was diagnosed with kidney failure. I had to leave my job, I lost my sight along the way, and in April 2025 I had a kidney and pancreas transplant.

My wife was beside me through all of it, taking me to every appointment, caring for me daily and lifting me when I struggled, all while working full time. Leeam Applications is my way of giving something back to her.

I always wanted to run my own business, but I never knew what it would be. The idea only came to me after I lost my sight. It showed me how inaccessible the world can be, and I want to help change that, even in a small way. Learning to use a computer with a screen reader, and to work with AI, has given me purpose and independence again.

Before I lost my sight I played snooker and pool in competitions around the country. I have always loved cars and motorsport, and I still never miss a Formula 1 race.

Lee

Hello I'm Lee,

My journey has been anything but linear, taking me from retail and warehousing to working as a vehicle mechanic and in security. Across all these diverse roles, I realised I was always most fulfilled when helping others, whether stepping up as a union representative or training colleagues. That natural drive took a backseat when chronic back pain hit in 2017, eventually leading to job loss, mobility aids, and a subsequent battle with a panic disorder, depression, and anxiety compounded by the isolating years of COVID-19.

Coming through those dark times gave birth to Leeam Applications. Running my own business has long been a dream, but creating applications that genuinely support and uplift people is deeply personal to me; knowing I've made someone's day a little easier beats any standard nine-to-five. On a lighter note, people might be surprised to learn that I am an avid paranormal investigator who loves exploring haunted locations and UFO-spotting in the Scottish Highlands.
