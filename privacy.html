Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to your information when you visit this website, leeamapplications.com. It is written in plain English because we think privacy notices should be easy to read.

Who we are

Leeam Applications is an independent app studio in the United Kingdom. If you have any question about this notice, email us at info@leeamapplications.com.

What this notice covers

This notice covers this website only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, because an app can do things a website does not. The privacy policy for RYX is at ryxapp.com/privacy.html.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no trackers and no adverts. The fonts it uses are stored on the website itself, so nothing is loaded from a font service or any other third party when you visit. We do not know who you are, and we do not want to.

Where the website is hosted

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub. When you visit, GitHub may process your IP address in its server logs for security reasons, for example to protect against attacks. We do not see or keep those logs. You can read how GitHub handles this in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email us

If you send us an email, we use your email address and what you wrote only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not share your details with anyone else. We keep the conversation only for as long as we need it to help you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR, you have rights over your personal information. You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, and ask for it to be deleted. To use any of these rights, email info@leeamapplications.com and we will help.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date below.

Effective date: 14 October 2026.
