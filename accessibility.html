Accessibility at Leeam Applications

Accessibility is the reason Leeam Applications exists. One of us is blind and uses a screen reader every day, so everything we make is built and tested with a screen reader from the very first day.

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.2, at level AA. It works with screen readers such as VoiceOver, TalkBack and NVDA. It can be used with a keyboard alone. It stays readable when you zoom in or make the text larger, and its colours have strong contrast. The main text uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed by the Braille Institute so every letter is easy to tell apart.

If anything on this website, or in any of our apps, is hard to use, please tell us at info@leeamapplications.com. We read every message, and your feedback helps us make things better for everyone.
